Last updated: August 1, 2026 · City Legacy Bank

1. Eligibility

To apply for a Personal, Business, or Mortgage loan you must be at least 18, provide accurate information, have a valid email and phone, and agree to open a City Legacy checking account for disbursement if approved.

2. Application & account creation

Submitting an application creates a City Legacy customer account and a pending loan application. Credentials are shown only after successful submission. Keep them confidential.

3. Credit assessment & decisions

We may review credit history, income, employment or business status, and other information. Approval is not guaranteed. Decisions are typically within 24–48 hours after identity verification. Additional documents may be requested.

4. Loan amounts, rates & repayment

  • Personal: $1,000 – $100,000 subject to approval.
  • Business: Up to $250,000 subject to approval.
  • Mortgage: Custom terms based on property, down payment, credit, and income.

Rates, fees, and schedules are disclosed in your formal offer before acceptance. Late payments may incur fees and affect credit.

5. Disbursement

Approved funds go only to your City Legacy account after verification and acceptance of the final agreement.

6. Your responsibilities

  • Provide true, complete information.
  • Use proceeds only for the stated purpose.
  • Pay on time per the agreed schedule.
  • Update us if contact or financial circumstances change materially.

7. Default & remedies

Failure to pay or material breach may constitute default. We may accelerate the balance, report to credit bureaus, and pursue collection as permitted by law.

8. Equal opportunity

City Legacy Bank is an Equal Housing Lender and does not discriminate on protected characteristics.

9. Changes & governing law

Terms may be updated; the version at application applies. Governed by the laws of the jurisdiction where City Legacy Bank is organized.

10. Contact

Questions: contact support through online banking or details provided after account creation.
